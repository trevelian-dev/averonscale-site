What I do

The whole ad side of the brand, every month.

01

Ads made from your content

Your shoots, your fit pics, your customers. Phone photos sell clothes; the best ad on this site is a tee on a parking garage floor.

02

Run daily

Budget moves to whatever is selling cheapest. Ads that don't sell get cut, and new ones go in built off what did.

03

Every sale tracked

Pixel and Conversions API set up on your store, so Meta learns who actually buys and not just who clicks.

04

Planned around your drops

List building in the weeks before, your warmest people first on drop day, and cart reminders after.

05

A report every Monday

What you spent, what sold, and what each sale cost. Ad spend stays on your own card, so you always see it.

Who it's for

Brands that make the clothes and want someone on the ads.

You have a store, a look, and product people like when they see it. You're boosting posts, or you ran ads once and they burned money. You want someone who knows clothing to run it, and you don't want to give up a percentage of every sale to do it.
