Questions

Straight answers.

Who is this for?+

Streetwear and clothing brands with a store, either running ads already or ready to start. If you make the clothes and want someone else to run and make the ads, this is for you.

How does pricing work?+

One flat monthly fee. No percentage of your sales, no cut of your profit. Your ad spend is separate and goes straight to Meta from your own card.

Do you hold my ad money?+

Never. You add us to your ad account and the spend comes off your own card, so you always see and control it.

What do I need to do?+

Add us to your ad account and send photos and video from your shoots and your customers. We make the ads from those, run them, and send you a report every Monday.

How fast do ads go live?+

First ads are live within days of you adding us to the ad account and sending your content.

Do I have to get on a call to get the plan?+

No. Send your Instagram and your email and the plan comes to your inbox. The 15 minute call is only if you want to walk through it together.
