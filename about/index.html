About

It started with my own brand.

The ads that sell clothes look like the brand’s own Instagram.

I'm Trevelian Curry. I learned Meta ads by running them for Synotacy, my own streetwear label, with my own money on the line.

After that I ran paid ads at a creative agency, for runway shows, clothing brands and local businesses. What I took from all of it: the ads that sell clothes look like the brand's own Instagram, not like an ad.

Averon Scale is that work, for clothing brands only. One flat fee, your card on the ad account, your content in the ads, and a report every Monday that tells you what each sale cost.

If you want to know whether ads would work for your brand, send me your Instagram and I'll tell you straight.
