Privacy

Your information, and what we do with it.

Last updated November 22, 2026

Averon Scale runs and makes Meta ads for streetwear and clothing brands. This page explains what information we collect when you ask for a free ads plan or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

Only what you give us, through a form on Facebook or Instagram, the form on this website, or a message you send us:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your brand's Instagram handle and website
  • Rough figures you choose to share, such as monthly sales and ad spend
  • When you would like to start

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta. It tells us when someone visits the site or asks for a plan after seeing one of our ads, so we can measure those ads and show them to people who have visited. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

How we use it

  • To build the ads plan you asked for
  • To contact you about it by text, call, email or Instagram message
  • To follow up about working together, if you are interested

By giving us your phone number you agree that we may text or call you about your request. Reply STOP to any text and we will stop.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other brands. It passes through the services we use to run the business: Meta (the lead form and the Meta Pixel), and Google (this website's form, email and spreadsheets). Each handles it under its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are in touch and for up to 24 months after our last contact, then delete them. Ask us and we will delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Email hello@averonscale.com and we will handle it within 30 days.
