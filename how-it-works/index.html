Questions
How much does it cost?

One flat monthly fee, set on the call once I've seen your numbers and how much work your brand needs. It's never a percentage of your sales. Ad spend is separate and goes from your card straight to Meta.

Why no percentage of sales?

A percentage takes more of your margin the better you do, and it pays me for sales the ads didn't cause. A flat fee keeps it simple: the ads either sell at a cost that works for you or they don't.

How much should I spend on ads?

Meta's own guidance is about 50 sales a week per ad set for it to finish learning. Most new brands can't fund that on day one, so we start with fewer, stronger ads and grow the budget as sales come in. The calculator above shows your break-even.

Do you make the ads too?

Yes. They're made from your own photos and video: edits, statics and carousels. If you don't have much content, the plan says exactly what to shoot on a phone.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. Plenty of brands come in boosting posts. You need a store that takes orders and products people want; the rest gets set up with you.

Not sure ads will work for your brand?

Send me your Instagram. I'll look at your page, your store and any ads you've run, and send back a plan for your next drop. Free, and no call needed.
